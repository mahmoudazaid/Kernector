## Role

Experienced AI Engineer to design, build, and deploy end-to-end AI-powered applications. The role covers Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), multi-agent architectures, and intelligent workflows, with ownership of the full lifecycle from concept to production.

## Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain end-to-end AI applications, from data ingestion and model integration to deployment and monitoring
- Build and optimize RAG pipelines, including document ingestion, embedding strategies, vector databases, and retrieval logic
- Design and implement multi-agent systems, coordinating autonomous agents to solve complex tasks
- Integrate LLMs and AI services into scalable, production-grade systems
- Develop robust backend services and APIs using Python
- Deploy, monitor, and scale AI solutions on Azure, leveraging cloud-native services
- Ensure reliability, security, observability, and performance of AI systems in production
- Collaborate with product managers, data scientists, and software engineers to translate business needs into technical solutions
- Stay up to date with emerging AI frameworks, protocols, and best practices

## Required skills

- Strong proficiency in Python for production-grade software development
- Proven experience building AI/LLM-based applications (RAG, agents, conversational systems)
- Solid understanding of the end-to-end AI lifecycle: data ingestion, preprocessing, model usage, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring
- Hands-on experience with Azure Cloud, including deploying and operating AI services
- Knowledge of key AI communication and orchestration protocols, such as MCP and A2A
- Experience designing APIs and backend services (REST, async systems, streaming where applicable)
- Familiarity with vector databases, embeddings, and information retrieval techniques
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work autonomously on complex systems

## Nice to have

- Experience with multi-agent frameworks and orchestration patterns
- Familiarity with MLOps / LLMOps practices
- Experience with security, access control, and compliance in AI systems
- Exposure to frontend integration or full-stack AI applications

## Offer

The salary range is €75,000 to €85,000 gross per year, depending on experience, skills, and seniority. Level and classification follow the Metalmeccanici Industria collective agreement (CCNL). The figure is Gross Annual Salary (RAL) only. The package may also include variable pay tied to individual or company objectives, plus welfare programs and benefits.

BIP also describes agile work up to 100% of the time, more than 300 training courses, and inclusion under Italian Law 68/99.

## Selection process

CV review, then typically an HR interview, a technical interview with a line manager, and a possible interview with a Partner. Order and number of interviews can vary.
