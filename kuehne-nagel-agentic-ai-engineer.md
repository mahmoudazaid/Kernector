## Role

Design, build, and optimize autonomous AI agents that improve decision-making, automate workflows, and integrate with logistics systems across Sea Logistics. The work is intelligent automation that stays safe, reliable, scalable, and practical for real operations.

## How you create impact

- Design and develop autonomous AI agents with reasoning, planning, decision-making, and task-execution capabilities
- Build agentic AI solutions using frameworks such as LangChain, AutoGPT, CrewAI, ReAct, or custom architectures
- Integrate AI agents with enterprise systems, including TMS, ERP, CRM, WMS, APIs, databases, and event-driven platforms
- Build safety and control mechanisms, including guardrails, validation layers, fallback logic, and human-in-the-loop processes
- Develop monitoring, logging, and explainability capabilities for transparency, reliability, and compliance
- Optimize and validate agent performance for accuracy, reliability, latency, and operational cost through benchmarking and real-world scenario testing
- Identify and prototype new agentic AI use cases across customer service, operations, planning, and reporting
- Collaborate across teams to translate business needs into scalable technical architectures and automation solutions

## What they ask for

- 3+ years building agentic AI systems and integrating them with enterprise APIs, databases, and business or logistics platforms
- Strong Python engineering skills and experience with at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
- Strong understanding of RAG pipelines, prompt engineering, model tuning, and LLM orchestration
- Experience with AI workflow automation and risk management: guardrails, validation layers, fallback mechanisms, and other safety controls
- Familiarity with event-driven systems, SQL/NoSQL databases, APIs, and distributed applications
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
- Freight forwarding or logistics knowledge is preferred and can be learned on the job
- Problem-solving and collaboration skills, and the ability to turn business challenges into practical, scalable technical solutions

## Offer

Impact on logistics and IT, a global team, individual career planning, and support from team leaders. Teamwork, continuous learning, and diversity are stated as part of the environment.
